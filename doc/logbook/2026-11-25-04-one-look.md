# 2026-11-25 — One look

The reviewer confirmed the two fixes of the entry before, and both of the guards that entry could only catch
in pairs: each fails a probe of the reviewer's own that holds a request in the interval the suite cannot. Two
more cases of a stop arriving in the middle of something followed, and both are the same mistake: state looked
at twice, with the stop between the looks. Fixed here. The counts are of the gateway's, the follower's, the
lifecycle and the session tests, 88 of them, run against each removal alone.

## A retired handle, looked up after the stop

The router found its run live, and then the handle a request named was looked up in the gateway's state as it
was by then. A stop empties the routes and the retirements. A `tasks/get` under a retired handle, refused
before the stop and after it, was in between left to this JVM, whose registry can still hold a task under the
handle, possibly the other conversation's.

The state is now read once: the run is found live in it, and the same reading says where the request's
handles go. Nothing is held while the request is served. The test takes that reading with a handle retired,
stops the gateway, and asks where a task under the handle goes, by the reading and by a fresh one; the fresh
one says here, which is the finding. A handle looked up in the state as it is by then: 1 failure.

## A wait, ended by the last run's welcome

An attach wrote its welcome and then told whoever was waiting for a node of its name, without asking whether
it was still that node. Held between the two across a restart, it took a wait made on the second run and
settled it with nothing, there being no such node; the caller then missed the node it was waiting for.

The waits are now collected, with the roster line they are settled with, under the lock a node is forgotten
under, and only when the attach is still its name's node. They are settled after the lock is let go. The test
holds a real attach after its welcome, restarts the gateway, waits for the name, and lets the attach go: the
wait is still on, and is ended by the node that attaches to the second run. Settled whoever waits: 2 failing
assertions.

## The rest of the role, read for the same mistake

- What a follower says between requests is acted on under the lock, after asking again there that its entry
  is still its name's node. An entry belongs to one run, so that covers a restart.
- A request is queued for an entry and the entry then looked up again; one gone in between is answered as
  gone. An entry of an ended run is gone.
- Minting in the gateway's own JVM asks the fleet through a hook that a stop removes.

Nothing further was found there. That is a reading, not a probe.
